Activity 2: Binary Subtraction

1. Subtract 1 from 111011101011:

  111011101011
−           1
-------------
  111011101010

→ Result in decimal:
Binary: 111011101010 = 0b111011101010 = 3818

2. Subtract 1110101 from 111011101011:

  111011101011
−     1110101
-------------
  111010011110

→ Result in decimal:
Binary: 111010011110 = 0b111010011110 = 3790
