Activity 2: Base 10 to Base 8
75 (base 10) = 113 (base 8)

Steps:
- 75 ÷ 8 = 9 remainder 3
- 9 ÷ 8 = 1 remainder 1
- 1 ÷ 8 = 0 remainder 1 → reverse remainders: 113
