Activity 1: Binary Addition

1. Add 1 (binary: 1) to 111011101011 (binary):

  111011101011
+           1
-------------
  111011101100

→ Result in decimal:
Binary: 111011101100 = 0b111011101100 = 3820

2. Add 111011101011 to 111011101011:

  111011101011
+ 111011101011
-------------
 1110111010110

→ Result in decimal:
Binary: 1110111010110 = 0b1110111010110 = 7638
